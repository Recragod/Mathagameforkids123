Addition (+)

Addition is combining two or more numbers together to get a new total. The symbol used in addition problems looks like a cross, +, and is called a plus sign. The total for addition is called the sum.

For example, you can have 3 cars and if your father gives you 2 more, you will then have 5 cars.

We can express this as: 3 + 2 = 5

Example:

24 + 13 = 37

10 + 25 = 35
