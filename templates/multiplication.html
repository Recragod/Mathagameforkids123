Multiplication (×)

Multiplication is where you take one number and add it to itself a certain number of times

For example, you and your 3 bestfriends decided to buy 3 cars each. If 4 people gets 3 cars each, that is 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 cars.

However, we can express this as: 4 × 3 = 12

Example:

12 × 3 = 36

9 × 3 = 27
