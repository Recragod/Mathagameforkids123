Subtraction (−)

Subtraction is the process of taking one number away from another. It helps us figure out how much is left, how far apart two numbers are, or how many remain after part of a group is removed.

For example, you can have 5 but your best friend who you thought would stay by your side forever, betrays you by taking 3 cars away, leading to severe depression. You will now have 2 cars!

We can express this as: 5 - 3 = 2

Example:

74 - 7 = 67

45 - 3 = 42
