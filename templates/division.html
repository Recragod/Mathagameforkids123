Division (÷)

Division in maths is the process of breaking a number up into equal parts, and finding out how many equal parts can be made.

For example, you can have 6 cars in you garage. But your three bestfriends decided to steal all of it and share it equally among themselves. So each of them got 2 cars.

We can express this as: 6 ÷ 3 = 2

Example:

66 ÷ 33 = 2

9 ÷ 3 = 3
